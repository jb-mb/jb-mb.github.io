Matching Brackets will be back the first of 2027!

LinuxYouTube

After this Friday's video, I'm going to take a break from publishing new videos for the remainder of the year.

I'll be spending some time working on the Matching Brackets website, revisiting the direction of the channel, and figuring out how to make things better going forward.

Don't worry, Matching Brackets isn't going anywhere! I just think it's time to step back, regroup, and work on a few things behind the scenes.

Thanks to everyone who's been watching, commenting, and supporting the channel. I appreciate it!

I'd like to wish everybody a joyous holiday season and a happy new year.

See you in 2027!
