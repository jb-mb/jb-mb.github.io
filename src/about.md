  <section class="prose-section">
    <h2>Why “Matching Brackets”?</h2>
    <p>In programming, an opening bracket needs a matching closing bracket. When the pieces fit together, the code makes sense. When they don’t, it can be hard to find what went wrong.</p>
    <p>Learning technology can feel the same way. There are a lot of pieces to understand, but when they fit together, things become clearer and using your computer becomes more enjoyable. That’s what Matching Brackets is all about: helping Linux make sense.</p>
